# Code of Conduct

WildSource is a place for people who care about nature and open software. We want everyone to feel welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect wildlife and the people who share their cameras.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Submitting streams that show identifiable people without consent, or that expose the location of nests or at-risk species.
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, streams or contributions that violate this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously violate it.

## Reporting

Report problems by [opening an issue](https://github.com/jasanpreetn9/wildsource/issues/new) on the WildSource repository, or by mentioning the maintainer, [@jasanpreetn9](https://github.com/jasanpreetn9).

If the report is sensitive, don't put the details in a public issue. Open an issue that only asks the maintainer to contact you privately, and they will reply to arrange a private channel. Reports are kept confidential.
